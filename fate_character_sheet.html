Notes

How it works. A sheet auto-saves in this browser once it has a name. Renaming a sheet renames its save. Copy duplicates the current sheet. Print makes an 8×5 inch card, and two fit on a US Letter page. Print Blank prints an empty character card and an empty NPC card. Export downloads every sheet as a JSON file, and Import merges sheets from one. A sheet with the same name is replaced. Nothing leaves your browser.

Stress follows Fate Core. Physical and mental stress start with two boxes. An Average or Fair Physique (or Will) adds a third box, and Good or better adds a fourth. The sheet works out your boxes from the skill pyramid and shows the ones you don't have as dashed. A Superb (+5) Physique or Will, which you can only reach through advancement, also gives an extra mild consequence; use the fourth consequence line for it. Each box absorbs as many shifts as its number. For how stress and consequences play out, see Actions, Outcomes & Conflicts; for building the character, see Character Creation.
